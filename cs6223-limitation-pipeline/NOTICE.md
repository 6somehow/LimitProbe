## What was removed for publication

- Paper PDFs and the raw model-request logs that contained full paper text (`paperside/handoffs/**/evidence`).
  The teammate's audit scripts that read them (`validate_handoff_archive.py`, `method-limitations/pilot.py`) need
  the PDFs fetched again.
- Agent task packages, dispatch scripts and harness notes (the development scaffolding).
- Compiled third-party libraries recorded inside two test attempts.
- The author's machine paths. Recorded outputs now contain placeholders: `<project-root>`, `<limitset-raw>`,
  `<limitset2-raw>`, `<python>`, `<conda-env>`, `<workspace>` and `<home>`. To replay a recorded attempt,
  replace `<project-root>` with the path of your checkout.
