## The 7 lenses (second-order: paper fact + a general systems/ML premise -> a conditional consequence the paper did not state; headline form 'A does not imply/guarantee B' or 'A conflicts with B')
L1 Proxy != target property: the method optimizes or measures X, but the benefit depends on a different property Y.
L2 Objective / stopping-rule mismatch: the search or training objective (stop at the first valid result; imitate a teacher; self-generated feedback) differs from the real goal; diversity on one axis can hide concentration on another.
L3 Granularity / unit mismatch between two mechanisms: the method's unit vs the surrounding system's or ecosystem's unit (per-component vs shared group; protection unit vs scheduling unit; replica unit vs request unit; engineering boundary vs semantic dependency).
L4 Unnecessary binding / coupling: two things bound together that need not be (e.g. state compression and update direction; a cheap shared transform and the search space; low-frequency logic on the high-frequency path).
L5 Origin of the exploited structure: where does the regularity the method relies on come from (architecture, encoding, deployment condition) and which common change would move it?
L6 Cost moved, not removed / net-benefit threshold: the cost shifts in time, place or to co-tenants; the benefit needs a threshold (interval, hit rate, usefulness of prediction).
L7 Regime change over scale or time: what works early becomes the bottleneck later (as the model improves, as data or objects grow, as tenants share resources).
Category hint: applicability_limitation = holds only when an environment/workload/ecosystem condition holds (L1, L3, L6); mechanism_origin_unclear = the cause of the exploited pattern is not established (L5); method_shortcoming = the method's own objective/design limits it (L2, L4, L7). Disclosure values: reported | mechanism_reported_consequence_inferred | inferred (some references are explicit author boundaries restated with the mechanism: include both kinds and label which).
